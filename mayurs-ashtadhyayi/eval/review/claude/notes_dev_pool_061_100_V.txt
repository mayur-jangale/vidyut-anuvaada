#! Nominal sentences: the subject is the clause root (कर्ता>-) and the predicate is विधेय>subject; with a copula the subject is कर्ता>copula verb. Object complements are विधेय>the object.
#! Passives (finite passive, passive क्त, gerundive, शक्य+infinitive): the nominative is कर्म, the instrumental agent is कर्ता, a genitive agent is सम्बन्ध; a क्त/कृत्य predicate is itself क्रिया>- (active कर्तरि क्त likewise).
#! Subordinate verbs head to the correlative word (relative clause verb > तद्/सः, यथा-clause verb > तथा), not to the main verb; quoted clauses head to the verb of saying/thinking, and इति > the last verb of the quote.
#! Qualifiers of an unexpressed subject (including the "you" of a 2nd-person verb) are विशेषण>the finite verb; a simile standard is विशेषण>the compared noun with इव>the standard.
#! The locative absolute is marked by case, not भावलक्षण: subject अधिकरण>participle, participle अधिकरण>main verb (genitive absolute here analogously सम्बन्ध); postpositions प्रति/सह/विना are अव्यय>their noun, and a sāpekṣa genitive attaches to the compound member it qualifies.
